        Design decisions

        Why the screens look the way they do

        1Browse freely, sign in at checkout

        Tourists arrive from Google on a phone. Asking for an account before they see a listing loses them; sign-in appears only when they book.

        2One listing page, in the order guests decide

        Hook → facts → story → impact → money → logistics → price & policy → reviews. On mobile a sticky bar replaces the desktop sticky booking box.

        3Trust signals before price

        Impact badge on every card, "Where your money goes" bar, verified host, and the cancellation policy written in plain words beside the price.

        4No surprises at checkout or cancel

        The guest service fee is its own line before paying. Cancelling shows the exact refund (policy × time left) before the guest confirms.

        5The host form mirrors the guest page

        Same 18-section order, one section per step, prompts and examples so rural hosts never face a blank page. Required sections block Submit; drafts save anytime.

        6Admin is queue-first

        The dashboard leads with what needs a person (approvals, disputes, holds, enquiries). Fees, policies, categories and content change without a developer.

        7Brand: endorsed by The Habitat Group

        Earthy colours from thehabitat.my, set in Inter; Lokali gets its own leaf wordmark. Colours are tokens, so the palette can change in one place.
