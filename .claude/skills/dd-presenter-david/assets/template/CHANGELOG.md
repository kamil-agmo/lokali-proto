# Changelog

The version shows under the tag in the presenter's left card (`version` in `shell.config.js`) and goes up on every deploy:

- **+0.0.1, small:** a fix, nudge or wording change (0.1.0 → 0.1.1)
- **+0.1, medium:** a layout change or a new screen (0.1.1 → 0.2.0)
- **+1.0, large:** a redesign or a new flow (0.2.0 → 1.0.0)

Newest first. One line per change, in words a viewer understands.
