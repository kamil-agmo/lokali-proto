# Requirements: details

## The questions, in full

| Header | Question | Options (with the explanation to show) |
|---|---|---|
| `Files` | Where are the requirement files? | **A folder or files on this computer (Recommended)**: give the path; every file in it is read (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text, images) · **Attached in this chat**: use the files already attached · **Links**: web pages or shared documents I can open (private Google/SharePoint links need a connector or an export) · Other: paste the text |
| `Research` | How much research beyond the documents? | **Documents + quick research (Recommended)**: also look up the domain, the rules that apply (e.g. payments, health, accessibility) and 3-5 similar apps for common patterns · **Documents only**: nothing outside the files; fastest, nothing invented · **Deep research**: also compare competitors feature by feature and collect design references (takes longer) |
| `Output` | What should come out of it? | **Design brief + user stories (Recommended)**: REQUIREMENTS.md and USER_STORY.md, ready for the prototype kit · **Brief + flows and screen list**: also user flows and a screen-by-screen inventory · **Brief, then start the prototype**: the brief, then run dd-prototype-kit-kamil with it |
| `Gaps` | When something is unclear or missing, what should I do? | **List them and suggest answers (Recommended)**: open questions go in the brief with a suggested answer, marked as an assumption · **Ask me now**: stop and ask before writing (up to 4 at a time, as options) · **Ignore unclear parts**: leave them out of the brief |

## The two output files

- `REQUIREMENTS.md`, in this order: Summary (5 lines: what, for whom, why, platform, success measure) · Users and roles (needs, context, devices) · Goals and success measures · Features by priority (MoSCoW table: feature, priority, tag, source) · Key flows (step lists, with error and empty cases) · Screen inventory (screen, purpose, main content, key actions, roles) — with "flows and screens" chosen, make this complete · Content and data (fields, formats, sample values) · Rules and constraints (business rules, legal and compliance, accessibility, languages, brand, tech or platform limits) · Design implications (recommended design system and why, density, text size for the audience, light/dark, admin or desktop screens needed) · Open questions and assumptions (each with a suggested answer) · Sources (every file and link used).
- `USER_STORY.md`: "As a …, I want …, so that …" stories with acceptance criteria, grouped by role and in priority order, each linked to its feature (and source).
