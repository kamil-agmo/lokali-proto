Shared screens (host onboarding, Become a host intro, host Today labels, empty states, help text) must fit **any** experience and any cause: "experience", "guests", "a cause you choose"; photos that read as hosting in general (a guided walk, hands at a craft, a seedling). No single host's name, listing, or number (no "Pak Rahim", "RM 85 → RM 72.25", "4,200 mangroves") and no green example boxes explaining sums.

**Why:** the user cut the green example boxes from the onboarding mock (2026-11-27): hosts do different things, so specific examples mislead and over-explain ("mansplaining").

**How to apply:** specific data belongs only on screens that are about that listing or person (experience page, booking, Earnings). Check in every MIMS. Related: [[mims]], [[eco-listings]], [[flow-logic]].
