## 3. Keep (prevents a mistake or a loss)

- g-chat: Keep payments on Lokali. Never send money to a host directly.
- h-bank: MYR to Malaysian banks only · Lokali never asks for a banking password (shortened).
- a-booking: refund amount notice before cancelling.
- h-booking: Frequent host cancellations are reviewed by the Lokali team.
- "Meet your guide", "Where your money goes": Lokali feature names, kept.

## 4. Not a problem (real people's words)

Chat messages, reviews, guest notes, quotes and the booking email (≈ 20 findings) are written by guests and hosts, so they stay as they are and get `data-copy-ok`. Category and company names ("The Habitat Group") are proper nouns; "&" becomes "and".
