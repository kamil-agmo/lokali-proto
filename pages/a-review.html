Mangrove replanting morning

Plant your own mangrove seedling with the village that has protected this river for 40 years.

2 · Quick facts

3 hours · 4–20 guests · Starts 8:00 AM · English, Malay · Moderate fitness (mud) · Age 8+

3 · Story

When the 2004 tsunami reached our coast, the mangroves took the blow so our houses didn't. Since then our kampung replants every month. You'll pull on boots, wade into the mud with us and plant a seedling with your name on its tag. We send you a photo of it in a year.

4 · Itinerary

8:00 Welcome tea at the jetty · 8:30 Nursery walk · 9:15 Planting in the mudflats · 10:30 Rinse, kuih and kopi · 11:00 End

6 · Meet your guide

Pak Rahim, born in Kampung Kuantan. "Why I started this: my father planted the trees you'll walk under."

7 · Impact story

Every seedling is logged with the state forestry office; 1,800 planted since 2023 with an 81% survival rate.
