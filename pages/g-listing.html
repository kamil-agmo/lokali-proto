The story

As the sun sets, the river goes quiet. We paddle into the mangroves with no engines and no spotlights. Then the berembang trees begin to blink, thousands of fireflies flashing together in perfect time.

Colonies like this are rare, fewer than 20 remain worldwide, and paddling keeps them undisturbed. It's gentle enough for children and first-timers, and couples love the stillness.

Most guests go silent when the first tree lights up, and talk about it all the way back home.

FAQs

What if it rains?

Light rain is fine. In heavy rain the host moves you to another date or you get a full refund.

Is it safe for young children?

Yes, from age 5. Child life jackets are provided and boats stay close to the bank.

Can I take photos of the fireflies?

Without flash only. Most phones can't capture them — the host shares a long-exposure photo after the trip.
