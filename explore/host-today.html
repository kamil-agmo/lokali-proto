What's on it, and why (MIMS)

From A

  • Cover photo = the host's own listing photo (any category). Greeting + "Next experience in 3 days" on it.
  • Next experience floats on the cover edge, like Home's search bar: date, time, booked / capacity, Guest list.

From B

  • Guest faces in that card: who's coming; dietary or access flags as a count ("1 allergy"), detail on the guest list.
  • Coming up: next dates with fill bars, taps to Calendar (capacity per slot, H8). Works for any schedule (weekly, one-off, retreats).
  • To do, after the impact band: new bookings, reviews to answer, payout on the way (H14). Urgent items already reach the host another way (booking email, Inbox badge), so the band can lead.
  • Impact band, same pattern as guest Home: money given back through the listing + guests hosted. Every listing has an impact share, so it's true for all hosts.

General wording

  • "Experience", "guests", "given back" everywhere; no class, meals, trees or kuih on Today.
  • RM 90 = 10% of her RM 905 from November's 2 experiences (sample).
